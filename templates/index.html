How it works

Three steps, about two seconds.

01

Snap or upload

Photograph your handwritten page, or drag an image straight onto the drop zone. Multiple pages at once is fine.

02

Claude reads it

Claude's vision model works through the handwriting — including cursive and the parts you'd struggle to read yourself.

03

Edit and take it with you

Fix anything that came out wrong, then copy it, download .txt or .docx, push it to Google Docs or Notion, or share a link.

Getting clean results

Four habits that do most of the work.

Tip 01

Light it properly

Daylight or a bright lamp. Dim photos are the single biggest cause of misread words.

Tip 02

Shoot straight down

Hold the camera parallel to the page so the text fills the frame without skewing at the edges.

Tip 03

Dark pen, plain paper

Contrast is everything. Ballpoint on unlined white gives you the fewest [illegible] markers.

Tip 04

Send whole notebooks

Upload several photos together. Each page is saved separately and stays searchable in your library.
