How it works

Three steps, about two seconds.

01

Snap or upload

Photograph your handwritten page, or drag an image straight onto the drop zone. Multiple pages at once is fine.

02

The handwriting gets read

A vision model works through the page — including cursive and the parts you'd struggle to read yourself.

03

Edit and take it with you

Fix anything that came out wrong, then copy it, download .txt or .docx, push it to Google Docs or Notion, or share a link.

The story

Why I built this

As a student, I loved taking notes on my computer — until I hit a strict "no device policy" in middle school. Back to pen and paper. Friends were copying class notes onto their devices at home every night. I looked everywhere for a solution — nothing affordable, nothing built for this.

Note-Cloud is that tool. It uses Claude's vision AI to read handwriting, so even messy notes can make it into your notes app, email, or wherever you need them.
