Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Note-Cloud ("we", "us") turns photos of handwritten notes into typed text and, optionally, sends that text to your Google Docs. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text.
  • Your content — the images you upload and the transcribed text produced from them.
  • Google account data — only if you choose to connect Google: an access/refresh token that lets us create and edit documents on your behalf, and your basic profile (name, email) if you sign in with Google.

How we use it

  • Uploaded images are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to perform the transcription you requested.
  • Transcribed text is stored in your account so you can view, edit, export, and manage it.
  • Google account data is used only to provide the features you ask for — creating or appending to the Google Docs you select.

Google user data

Note-Cloud's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Google data only to provide and improve the Google Docs features described here.
  • We do not transfer or sell Google user data to third parties, except as needed to provide the service or as required by law.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google data unless you explicitly ask us to for support, it's required for security or to comply with the law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized.

We request the narrowest scopes needed: permission to create/edit documents, and file-level Drive access limited to documents the app works with. You can disconnect Google at any time from your account settings, which deletes the stored tokens.

Storage and retention

Your data is stored on our servers for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes your transcriptions, notebooks, and stored Google tokens.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete individual transcriptions at any time.
  • Disconnect your Google account at any time.
  • Delete your entire account and associated data from settings.
